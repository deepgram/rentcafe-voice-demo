What Nora can help with

The leasing office and resident line, answered on the first ring

Nora takes the calls that tie up a leasing office — what's available, what does it cost, do you take dogs, can I see it Saturday — and the resident calls that follow: what do I owe, when's my lease up, when is maintenance coming. Anything that needs a person, she hands off.
